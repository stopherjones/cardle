About Cardle

Cardle is a simple, browser-based car guessing game. It is not a login page, shopping site, software installer, or account-verification page.

What this site does

Cardle lets you play two guessing games about cars: Cardle and CARtegories. The site runs entirely in your browser and does not require an account or any personal information.

Privacy

No sign-in is required. No personal data is collected or stored by the site. The only data used is the local game state in your browser while you play.

Safety

The site does not ask you to install software, download files, or disclose passwords or payment details. If you see a warning from Google, it is usually a review or reputation issue rather than an indication that the game is unsafe.

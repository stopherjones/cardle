How to Play

🏎️ Cardle (Standard Game)

Guess the secret vehicle in 8 guesses or fewer. The photo starts covered by 8 tiles; each guess reveals another tile to give you a clearer view. Attribute feedback (Make, Model, Country, Year) guides your next guess!

📊 CARtegories Game

Assign 4 vehicles from your pool into 4 category docks (Make, Model, Country, Year). Each vehicle can only be placed in one category dock. Try to score maximum points across all 4 categories!

🕹️ Daily vs Random Play

Both games offer Daily Play (same challenge for everyone globally, resetting at 00:00 UTC) and Random Play (unlimited randomized rounds).

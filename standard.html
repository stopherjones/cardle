How to Play: Cardle

🎯 Objective

Identify the secret vehicle in 8 guesses or fewer!

🧩 Image Tile Reveals

The vehicle photo starts covered by 8 tiles. Each wrong guess reveals another tile, giving you a clearer view of the car!

🎨 Clue Feedback

Each guess provides attribute feedback:

Ford Green: Exact match
Mustang Grey: Incorrect
1965 ▲ Yellow: Within 10 years (▲ newer / ▼ older)

🕹️ Game Modes

  • Daily Play: Same target car and tile reveal order for everyone every day. Resets at 00:00 UTC.
  • Random Play: Play unlimited random car challenges anytime!

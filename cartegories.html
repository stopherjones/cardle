How to Play: CARtegories

🎯 Objective

Assign the four vehicles from your pool into the four category docks to score maximum points across all categories!

🏎️ Categories & Docking

Select a car from the pool and place it into one of the four category docks:

  • 🏷️ MAKE: Enter the manufacturer of the assigned car.
  • 🚗 MODEL: Enter the exact model name of the assigned car.
  • 🌍 COUNTRY: Enter the origin country of the assigned car.
  • 📅 YEAR: Enter the production year of the assigned car.

📊 Scoring

Each car can only be assigned to one category dock. Choose wisely! Points are awarded for correct category answers upon submitting all guesses.

🕹️ Game Modes

  • Daily Play: Same daily pool vehicles for all players. Resets at 00:00 UTC.
  • Random Play: Generate unlimited random vehicle pool rounds!
